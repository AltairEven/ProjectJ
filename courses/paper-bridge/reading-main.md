# The Paper Bridge

素材版本：1.1.0；自编于2026年10月11日；课程L-C2026-01-001／1.9.0。未测得Lexile值、未获RAZ分级。

Mia has a toy car.
She wants a bridge for her car.
She puts two books on the table.
There is a space between the books.

Mia puts a piece of paper on the books.
The paper makes a bridge.
She puts her car on the paper.
The paper bends. The car falls.

“Can I make a new bridge?” says Mia.
She folds a new piece of paper.
She puts it on the same books.
She tries the same car again.

This time, the car stays on the bridge.
Mia smiles.
She draws the two bridges for Dad.
“This one holds my car,” she says.
