# The Lighthouse

素材版本：1.0.0；自编于2026年11月19日；只供课堂判断参考题集2.3.0使用（知识页文体），不进课程库。

A lighthouse is a tall tower.
It stands on rocks near the sea.
At the top, there is a big lamp.

At night, the lamp turns around and around.
Its light shines far over the water.
Ships see the light from a long way off.

The light tells the ships, "Rocks are here."
The ships stay away from the rocks.
They do not hit the rocks.

Long ago, a keeper lived in the lighthouse.
He climbed the stairs every evening.
He lit the lamp with oil.
Today, the lamp turns on by itself.
