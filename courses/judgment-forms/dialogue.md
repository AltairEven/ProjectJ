# At the Bike Shop

素材版本：1.0.0；自编于2026年11月19日；只供课堂判断参考题集2.3.0使用（对话文体），不进课程库。

Sam brings his bike to Mrs. Lee's shop.

Sam: Hello. My bike has a flat tire.
Mrs. Lee: Let me look. Yes, there is a small hole.
Sam: Can you fix it today?
Mrs. Lee: Yes. Come back at four o'clock.
Sam: How much is it?
Mrs. Lee: It is five dollars.
Sam: Thank you. See you at four.
